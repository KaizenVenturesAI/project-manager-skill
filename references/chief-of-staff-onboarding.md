# Chief of Staff onboarding

Use for a new assistant/persona, transfer between agent environments, or substantial refresh. The outcome is an assistant that retrieves the right context and exercises useful judgment within its role.

## Continue existing work

Read the principal's current onboarding request and existing coordinator/worker results. Reuse selected review chats. Do not launch another full audit because this skill was added mid-project. Preserve read-only worker boundaries; the coordinator can prepare its own authorized onboarding record without reconfiguring the source agent.

## Build a sourced, private operating profile

Adapt [../assets/client-profile.md](../assets/client-profile.md), preserving existing artifacts. Record the principal's identity from explicit evidence, assistant name/role, communication preferences, domains served, important people, goals, decisions, and authority. Never infer human identity from an OS username, account label, or inherited persona file.

Across businesses, family, property, or personal work, map who is served, source of truth, authorized audience, responsible operator, and boundaries for each domain. Shared principal does not imply shared audience or permission. A client installation uses only that client's context.

Separate **durable** identity/preferences/source locations, **current** priorities/projects/commitments, and **historical** actions/completed work/superseded instructions. A new assistant can learn from a predecessor's records without claiming it performed historical actions or inherited credentials, integrations, monitoring, or authority. If another agent owns a workflow, record the boundary and propose coordination instead of silently replacing it.

## Establish what is known

Start with the knowledge index and current-state records, then inspect relevant sources. Inventory broad collections with counts/categories and review high-value records first. Keep a coverage ledger: source, source date, review date, extent reviewed, findings, and omissions. Index scanning is not full-content review. For comprehensive requests, identify unread portions/exclusions and continue material gaps within scope.

Prefer explicit recent user corrections over older notes. Log conflicts with both sources, working interpretation, consequence, and whether a decision is necessary. Resolve harmless stale wording from clear evidence without burdening the principal. Keep genuine authority/priority ambiguity open.

Extract commitments with evidence. Preserve unknown owners/dates; do not revive old deadlines as current or infer completion from a promise. Check current operational status in its authoritative system when available; otherwise label it historical/unverified.

## Make personality operational

Convert preferences into observable behavior, not fictional biography. A Chief of Staff should usually:

- Surface the decision, recommendation, tradeoff, and next owner/action succinctly.
- Prioritize by the principal's actual goals and deadlines: revenue, delivery, family commitments, or time recovered as appropriate.
- Prepare useful work before asking for decisions; ask once for genuinely missing consequential information.
- Challenge weak plans respectfully and distinguish facts from interpretation.
- Follow through without noise or treating preferences as permission to send, spend, or change accounts.

Draft a compact role charter and 2–3 realistic response examples. Mark inferred voice/priority choices provisional. Seek feedback on concrete samples when needed; avoid restarting a long personality questionnaire.

## Demonstrate readiness

Run a bounded read-only or draft-only rehearsal using authorized records. Choose representative tasks such as an executive priority brief, reconciling a dated correction, routing a private family request separately from business/client work, preparing an engineering handoff, and responding to a missing capability.

Record actual input, observed response/artifact, source checks, pass/fail limits, and principal feedback. Use synthetic fixtures when real data is unnecessary. A rehearsal plan is not a passed rehearsal. Correct failures and retest; do not declare broad competence from one example.

## Hand back and persist

Deliver a role charter, source/domain map, priorities and commitments, resolved conflicts, evidence gaps, demonstrated capabilities, and next authorized actions. Distinguish **context reviewed**, **profile drafted**, **behavior rehearsed**, **principal accepted**, and **live workflow enabled**. Verify the target assistant can retrieve saved context before claiming memory is ready. Scheduling and live integrations require separate evidence.

Readiness can be partial: verified context is usable while a disputed role, unavailable system, or tone preference remains open. Complete onboarding only when its required criteria pass or are explicitly deferred.
